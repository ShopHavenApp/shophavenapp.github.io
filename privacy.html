Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Prelaunch notice: ShopHaven is in development and is not yet publicly launched. This policy describes intended data practices and must be confirmed against the final app, payment integration, and service providers before accepting live customers.

1. Who we are

ShopHaven is a marketplace platform being developed to let users browse listings, purchase eligible goods, and create and manage their own shops. For privacy questions, contact shophavenapp@gmail.com.

2. Information we may collect

  • Account information: email address, login credentials or authentication identifiers, profile information, and account settings.
  • Marketplace information: shop names, listings, product descriptions and images, transaction and order records, reviews, ratings, messages, and dispute submissions.
  • Seller onboarding information: eligibility confirmations and the status of identity or business verification performed by payment providers.
  • Technical information: device and app information, security events, diagnostic logs, and usage data needed to operate and protect the service.
  • Support communications: messages and information provided when contacting ShopHaven about an account, order, or dispute.

Actual collection depends on which features are enabled. We do not intend to collect full payment card numbers directly through ShopHaven's own servers; payment processing is intended to be handled by Stripe or another disclosed payment provider.

3. How information may be used

We may use information to create accounts and shops, display listings, enable marketplace interactions, process and document transactions, communicate with users, handle support and disputes, prevent fraud and abuse, maintain security, meet legal obligations, and improve the service.

4. When information may be shared

Information may be shared with the relevant buyer or seller when needed to fulfill an order or resolve a dispute; with payment and identity-verification providers such as Stripe; with hosting, database, authentication, and technical service providers; or with authorities when legally required. Public shop profiles, listings, and reviews may be visible to other users. We do not intend to sell personal information to data brokers.

5. Payments

ShopHaven plans to use Stripe Connect for seller payment onboarding and marketplace transactions. Stripe may independently collect and process identity, bank account, and payment information under its own policies. Learn more at Stripe's Privacy Policy. Live payment processing is not yet publicly available.

6. Retention and security

We intend to retain personal information only as reasonably needed to operate the marketplace, meet legal and accounting requirements, resolve disputes, and protect against misuse. We plan to use access controls and other reasonable technical safeguards. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable law and legitimate retention requirements. Some account and public profile controls may also be available inside the app once released.

8. Children

ShopHaven is intended for adults who meet the platform's seller eligibility and account requirements. We do not intend to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information.

9. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy as ShopHaven's features and operations develop. The updated version will be posted here with a new date. Material changes will be communicated where required by law.
