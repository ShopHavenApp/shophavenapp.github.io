Users are expected to provide accurate account information, safeguard their sign-in credentials, and comply with applicable laws. Sellers must meet the platform's age and eligibility requirements, complete required onboarding, and maintain accurate business and payment information.

Sellers are expected to describe products accurately, set truthful prices, fulfill orders as agreed, comply with product safety and consumer protection laws, and respond promptly to customer questions and complaints.
